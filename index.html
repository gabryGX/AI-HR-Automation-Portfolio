How It Works (6 Connected Workflows)

  1. Recruitment 1 — Intake: Validates candidate details and PDF integrity, prevents duplicate applications, saves CVs to Google Drive, and logs entries to Google Sheets.
  2. Recruitment 2 — AI Scan: Runs hourly to extract CV text (with Gemini OCR fallback for scanned PDFs), scores candidate fit via Groq LLMs, emails interview invitations or polite rejections, and briefs HR.
  3. Recruitment 3 — Error Handler: Captures workflow failures, throttles duplicate alerts, logs incidents, sends daily digests, and runs a 2-hour heartbeat check on stuck applications.
  4. HR Policy RAG Assistant: Answers employee questions strictly from indexed HR policy documents with source citations and conversation memory.
  5. Policy Loader: Automatically syncs policy docs from Google Drive into the vector store on n8n startup and daily at 7:00 AM.
  6. Job Application Coach: Orchestrates three specialist AI agents (CV Matcher, Cover Letter Writer, Interview Coach) to email a complete career report.
